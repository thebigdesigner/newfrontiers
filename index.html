About Us

Our Story: Born out of a divine burden to mentor and father a new generation of leaders, NFMGN is an incubator for ministers who are hungry for a shift and continued relevance in the discharge of their God-given assignments. Special attention will also be paid to balancing family life and ministry.

The “Frontier” Philosophy: We believe the minister of the future must be as sound in the Spirit as they are savvy in leadership and technology.

Who is this for?

Lead Pastors and Church Founders.

For pastors and ministry leaders who desire more than growth—those committed to building strong, grounded disciples, not just attendees. This network equips you with biblical depth, leadership clarity, and practical frameworks to disciple believers who can run with vision, multiply impact, and steward God's work with excellence and integrity.

Youth and Campus Ministers.

For leaders shaping the next generation in churches, campuses, and youth movements. If you're passionate about raising spiritually rooted, purpose-driven young believers who can stand firm in today's world, this network provides the tools, teachings, and spiritual formation needed to disciple leaders beyond excitement into maturity.

Marketplace Apostles and Creative Ministers.

For believers called to influence culture, industry, and creativity with the Kingdom of God. Whether you're an entrepreneur, professional, artist, media creator, or creative minister, this network helps you align your calling with sound doctrine, spiritual authority, and practical discipleship—so your work becomes a vessel for impact, not compromise.
